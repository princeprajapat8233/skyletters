# 🌌 Skyletters

*Some feelings are difficult to explain, but sometimes a picture says everything.*

Welcome to Skyletters!

I started this little project because I wanted to bring together a collection of paintings that carry different emotions, thoughts and stories.

The beautiful thing about art is that we don't always need words to understand it. Sometimes a painting reminds us of someone, sometimes it takes us back to a memory, and sometimes it makes us feel something we cannot really explain.

That's what I love about this collection.

### 🌙 About Skyletters

Skyletters is a collection of beautiful paintings created by **Meeraja Krishna**.

The artwork captures different feelings and moments through colours, skies, moons, stars and meaningful expressions.

I wanted to bring these paintings together in one place so that others could explore them, appreciate the details and perhaps find a little piece of themselves in the artwork.

### 🎨 About the Artwork

All the paintings featured in this collection are created by Meeraja Krishna.

I truly appreciate the creativity and emotions behind these artworks. Each painting has its own identity, and everyone might connect with it in a different way.

**Artist:** Meeraja Krishna

### ❤️ A Little Thought

I believe every painting holds a different story.

You might see a beautiful sky, someone else might see a memory, and another person might see a feeling they've never been able to express.

Maybe that's the beauty of art. It speaks differently to everyone.

Thank you for taking a little time to explore Skyletters.
